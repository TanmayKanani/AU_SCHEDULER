How to Use AU Scheduler

1. Get your AURIS data

  1. Log in to AURIS and open the Course Directory.
  2. Select the current semester (e.g. Winter 2027).
  3. Click the Excel or CSV export button. (Older AURIS versions export one file per school.)
  4. Back here, click Import AURIS and upload the file. If you have one file per school, import each of them.
  5. Half-term and short courses keep their dates (e.g. 4 Jan – 19 Feb), so two courses that share a time slot in different halves of the term are not treated as a clash.

2. Pin a preferred section

When a course has multiple sections, click Pin on the one you want. The generator will only consider that section, forcing it into every schedule it produces.

3. Save & compare versions

After generating, click Save Version to keep that combination. Saved schedules appear under the timetable; click Compare to view any two side-by-side with differences highlighted.

4. Share with friends

Open the ⋯ menu in the header → Share my courses to copy a code. Send it to a friend; they paste it via Import friend's code and instantly see your selections.

5. Semester credit target

Set your target credits in the dashboard. The app will refuse to add courses that exceed it, keeping you on track.
